## results
Consists of folders:

### 012_results_noContactSCS_opening_27M
- 300**3 grid res. with vacuum solid in Finger-T.tube connection to ensure opening.
- features Wakis WP and real and Im beam coupling Impedance result figures for respectivelly 30, 90, 100 and 120 cells skipped from the integrator.
-  2 screenshots of sigma and ieps checks
- no animations for simulation speed 

### 012_results_noContactSCS_PML_27M
- same as above but no screenshots (it's the same) of material masks AND uses PML boundary condition in the Z-domain.
- these are the final presented results (for 100 added cells)
- features animation


### 012_results_noContactSCS_opening_43M
- 350**3 grid resolution.
- cannot remember which boundary conditions were used if it was pec,pec,pec or pec,pec,pml however they were shown to yield similar results for the 6L2 300**3 results in the end
- data is there, but no figures (it's a wrong scaled figures in the folder, and shows results for an insuffiecient amount of added cells - probably only 20 added - and not 90/100.)
- no animation
- Added vacuum solid

### 012_results_noContactSCS_opening_125M
- 500**3 sgrid res.
- animation of the images of the simulated electric fields until the simulation was stopped (because the sim took approx. 12 hours on a workday HTCondor slot, so it was stopped within 1 hour.)
- includes two screenshots of the material masks
- no vacuum solkid added


### 125Mgridres_012_results_noContact_70cellsboundary
- features mask figures of the high grid resolution 500**3 mask of the 6L2 device
-- specifically: the Fingers with and without 0.2 boolean thresholding of SCS mask, and same for the 'Body'/316l object of the 6L2.
    --- for both of these sub-objects, zoomed figures are included, specifically on the bellows of the 6L2, which are fully resolved.

### first_benchmarks_cavity
features 4 figures of the benchmark of the meshing methods (legacy, interior points, implicit distance, voxelize_rectilinear)
- pyMesh bench4: volume accuracy on the cavity for the methods but before the ray casting methods (enclosed, interior) got an updated point-to-cell mask threshold, so here they still have same as legacy i.e. stl_tolerance
- pyMesh bench1: same as bench4 (sorry for the confusion of the numbers here), but where the ray casting methods got a new point-to-cell mask fixed threshold of 0.5 instead of the same as legacy.
- WP results on the cavity of all method, including voxelize_rectilinear with positive, negative and no shift
- Z results on the cavity of all method, including voxelize_rectilinear with positive, negative and no shift

### Shell_nocavity_bgVacuum_SCS_results
- Wakis simulation results on the cavity when the SCS mask without thresholding is used.
- features animation and material masks figures
- 60x60x140 grid res

### VolAcc_and_Times_methods_data
- data for respectivelly all objects of the 6L2 , all meshing methods in respect of volume accuracy, and also computing time
- same but with decimate_pro
- volue accuracy versus grid resolution data for the cavity object, referred to as shell in the file name.

### voxelize_rectilinear_objectMasks
- features a lot of mask figures, obtained with voxelize
- these are probably also featured in the CodiMD
- figures used to initially show Wakis team why to update meshing method to voxelize
